### Added
#### Dictionary Attributes
- Added `Proximity Card (12)`, `Smart Card (13)`, and `Mobile Credential (14)` enum values to `factor_type_id`, so physical access credentials presented at a reader can be carried as authentication factors.
- Added `Confirmed (28)`, `Unfounded (29)`, `Referred (30)`, `Cancelled (31)`, and `Unsubstantiated (32)` enum values to `disposition_id` for verdict-style outcomes of alarms and physical security dispatches.
- Added `Physical (14)` enum value to `logon_type_id` for an entry made at a door, gate, or turnstile reader.
